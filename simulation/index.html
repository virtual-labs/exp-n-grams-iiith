Instructions

▼
  1. Select either English or Hindi corpus from the dropdown.
  2. Read the corpus sentences shown in the left pane.
  3. Click Find Bigram Probabilities to generate the bigram table.
  4. Fill each cell with P(next_word | current_word) as a decimal value.
  5. Enter values only between 0 and 1.
  6. Click Check to validate your table and review colored cells.
  7. Use Show Answer to view the expected bigram probabilities.
  8. Use Reset to restart the simulation.
